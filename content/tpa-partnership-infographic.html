Insurance Informatics Operating Model
Input

What you need from us

We build the enrollment. We deliver it in your format.
You tell us the spec. We match it.
  • What file format do you prefer?
  • What fields do you require?
  • How do you prefer delivery? (SFTP / API / file drop)
  • What's your lead time to effective date?
  • How do you handle mid-year changes?
  • Can you share a sample enrollment file?
  • Clean, documented spec
  • Automated delivery
Middle

How you do your job

You handle the 90% routine. We handle the 10% high-dollar. Tickets come in as emails.
Your workflow stays yours. We send well-formatted data in, we need status back.
  • Typical adjudication turnaround?
  • Do you run a pre-cert program?
  • How do you flag high-dollar claims to us?
  • Can we configure custom flag thresholds?
  • What file feeds come back, and how often?
  • Service email inbox for tickets?
  • Status reporting back to our dashboard?
  • Open to working with our vendors?
  • Automated high-dollar flags
  • Email-based ticket intake
  • Regular status feeds
Output

How we close the loop

Vendor invoices paid on the claim side — same mechanism you already use.
We send the invoice. You process it as a claim. The client's funded account pays.
  • Can you process a vendor invoice as a claim?
  • What format do you need for invoices?
  • How do we submit pay-way bills?
  • What's the reconciliation cycle?
  • Payment turnaround (once account is funded)?
  • Can you share a sample claims report?
  • Vendor invoice on claim side
  • Clean reconciliation
The Funding Flow — We Have Your Back On This

The TPA can't pay until the employer funds the account.

When vendors call yelling about late payment, it's almost always a funding problem — the employer didn't wire the money into the claims account. You didn't do anything wrong. They forgot to fund.

Our operating model tracks the funded balance in real time. If it's running low, we alert the client before vendors start calling. You don't get caught in the middle of something the client controls.
