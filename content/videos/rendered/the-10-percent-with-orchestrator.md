## Production Notes

**The single-source notebook approach was deliberate and fixed a prior failure mode.** An earlier render in a different notebook ("The Integration Multiplier: Proving Industrial Scale for Vendors," notebook id `32a0a69b-acb5-4fc6-83f7-e637e2dda04d`) had 10 mixed sources including older Gate video material, fact-finder docs, and Maryland Health Commission reference material. NotebookLM's Video Overview synthesized across all of them and the output came back contaminated with sales content — four-step buyer funnel, fact finder, prospect-facing language. That render is documented in `rendered/insurance-informatics-explainer-REJECTED.md`.

For this video, the fix was:
1. **Rewrite the script** as a pure sequential 8-step walkthrough (SCRIPT v1.0 → v2.0, commit `9838220f`)
2. **Create a fresh NotebookLM notebook with ONLY the new script as the single source** — no older Gate material, no Tradesman case studies, no Cognizant research, nothing for NotebookLM to blend toward
3. **Use the Explainer format** (not Cinematic or Brief) — structured comprehensive overview
4. **Provide a strict customization prompt** that explicitly forbids sales content and lists the 8 steps in order

The result: NotebookLM's auto-generated source summary landed as *"operational mechanics of a centralized management hub... HR-branded communication... pre-vetted cases to appropriate specialty shops... consolidated billing and real-time reporting... technical guide for partners."* Zero sales signal. The single-source + explicit prompt approach is the locked pattern for future vendor operational explainers.

Dave reviewed the rendered video and confirmed "That video is great."
