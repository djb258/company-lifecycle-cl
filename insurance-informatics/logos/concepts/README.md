## My honest ranking (if I had to pick)

1. **#04 Lowercase domain-first** — Strongest pure-wordmark play. `insuranceinformatics.com` IS the logo. Fits your "the name is the brand" positioning perfectly. Stripe-school modern. Works at every size.

2. **#02 Swiss geometric** — Most timeless. Most enterprise-credible. Least likely to date. Works monochrome. Favicon-ready.

3. **#07 II monogram** — Only one with a standalone icon. Useful if you need a mark for favicon / social avatar that's not just the word. "II" ties back to Pattern of Twos / two bills / two sides. Subtle, not cutesy.

4. **#01 Editorial serif** — If you want maximum authority / institutional weight, this is it. Risk: serif wordmarks can feel slow-moving.

5. **#03 Stacked all-caps** — Distinctive but less versatile. Hard to use at small sizes.

6. **#05 Monospace** — Feels right for a data-discipline brand, but monospace wordmarks are a niche aesthetic — signal "tech startup" more than "insurance discipline."

7. **#06 Institutional** — Most conservative, most stodgy. Fine if you want to look like a 1960s consultancy.
